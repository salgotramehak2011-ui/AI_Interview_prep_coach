AI and Machine Learning Interview Preparation Guide

Supervised Learning: Uses labeled data to train models. Common algorithms include linear regression,
logistic regression, decision trees, random forests, SVM, and neural networks. Key metrics: accuracy,
precision, recall, F1-score, ROC-AUC.

Unsupervised Learning: Finds patterns in unlabeled data. Includes K-means clustering, hierarchical
clustering, PCA for dimensionality reduction, and autoencoders.

Deep Learning: Neural networks with multiple layers. CNNs for images, RNNs/LSTMs for sequences,
Transformers for NLP. Backpropagation and gradient descent are fundamental optimization techniques.

NLP: Tokenization, embeddings (Word2Vec, GloVe, BERT), attention mechanisms, fine-tuning LLMs.
Tasks include classification, NER, summarization, and question answering.

MLOps: Model versioning, CI/CD for ML, monitoring data drift and model performance, A/B testing,
feature stores, and reproducible pipelines with MLflow or Kubeflow.

Common Interview Topics: Bias-variance tradeoff, overfitting prevention (regularization, dropout,
early stopping), cross-validation, hyperparameter tuning, and ethical AI considerations.
